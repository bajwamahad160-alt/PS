# Arcanula: character sheet prompt (for Gemini)

**Author's direction:** she should look almost exactly like the author's
reference picture (Megicula's true devil form in the Black Clover manga), with
only small tweaks. Her outfit stays fully covered and modest.

**How to use it:**

1. Attach the author's **manga reference image** first (the black-and-white
   panel with the eye-covered wings).
2. Attach the same **art-style image** used for Owen, Jonah and Talia second.
3. Paste the prompt below.

```
Create a professional anime CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET / MODEL SHEET of one character: ARCANULA, the Devil of Gluttony, in FULL COLOR.

=== REFERENCES (VERY IMPORTANT) ===
The FIRST attached image is the DESIGN REFERENCE (a black-and-white manga panel). Arcanula should look ALMOST EXACTLY like the demon in it. Keep her design, silhouette and every major feature, turn it into a full-color character, and apply only the small TWEAKS listed below.
The SECOND attached image is the ART STYLE reference only: clean premium anime lineart, soft cel shading, muted elegant colors and faint paper grain. Do not copy its characters or clothes.

=== HER DESIGN (KEEP ALL OF THIS FROM THE REFERENCE) ===
- HAIR: a huge, wild, fluffy mane of pale, CURLY hair, big and voluminous around her head and shoulders, drifting slightly as if weightless.
- HORNS: two BLACK curled horns, like a ram's or goat's, sprouting from the top of her head, curving up and outward and then hooking back in at the tips.
- FACE: a pale, eerie, DOLL-LIKE face that looks almost like a porcelain mask. Wide round eyes set inside DARK SHADOWED RINGS, and a small, thin, calm smile. A small dark mark in the center of her forehead.
- COLLAR: a black choker at her throat with a ruffled, frilled collar (like an old-fashioned ruff) around her neck.
- BODY / GOWN: her whole body is a huge, full, flowing, TIERED gown made of layer upon layer of soft, rounded, FEATHER-LIKE or PETAL-LIKE scalloped panels, like a great cloak of pale feathers from her neck down to the ground. Very wide at the bottom. Small dark SYMBOLS are scattered across the feather-panels. It covers her completely from neck to feet. Only her face shows. No bare skin at all.
- WINGS: enormous, jagged, DARK wings spreading out behind her on both sides, wider than she is tall, with tattered, pointed edges. The wings are covered in many large EYE shapes (almond-shaped eyes with dark pupils), like a moth's or peacock's eye-spots.
- CHAINS OF WRITING: long, thick, floating CHAINS made of dense handwritten script, like ribbons of text, coil and loop around her body and drift through the air around her.
- Overall feeling: eerie, regal, ancient and unsettling. Beautiful and frightening at the same time, like a doll-faced queen of the dark.

=== THE TWEAKS (ONLY CHANGE THESE) ===
1. FULL COLOR PALETTE:
   - Hair: pale cream-white with a soft LAVENDER tint in the shadows.
   - Skin: pale porcelain with a faint cool grey-violet tint.
   - Horns: glossy black with a deep violet sheen.
   - Eye rings: deep plum-black.
   - Feather gown: soft ivory and pale lavender, with violet in the shadows between the layers.
   - Wings: deep black-violet. The eye-spots on the wings are GOLD with dark pupils, glowing softly.
   - Chains of writing: black ink script glowing faintly GOLD at the edges.
2. HER EYES: glowing GOLD irises with a thin VIOLET ring around each iris, inside the dark eye rings.
3. FOREHEAD MARK: change it to a small glowing GOLD symbol: a single CRESCENT MOON with a thin circular ring passing through it (like a small planet caught in a crescent's pull). This is the Gravity emblem.
4. GOWN SYMBOLS: change the small dark symbols on the feather-panels into small dark CRESCENT MOONS.
5. CHAINS: the chains of writing look like lines of handwriting torn out of an old magic book, as if the text of her griamore were wrapped around her.
6. GRAVITY TOUCH: a few small objects (a silver fork, a candle flame, a pearl, pebbles) float and slowly orbit around her, pulled toward her.
Everything else stays as close to the reference image as possible.

=== WHO SHE IS ===
Arcanula is an ancient demon of GLUTTONY bound inside a magic book of GRAVITY, locked away for sixty-one years in a dark, endless banquet hall of empty plates. She is calm, playful and eerie on the surface, and cold, ancient and hungry underneath.

=== SHEET LAYOUT ===
A wide landscape character sheet (16:9) on a dark charcoal-violet background with very faint grid lines, like a professional anime studio model sheet. Every view shows the SAME character in the SAME design and colors, hovering slightly above the ground at the same scale.

TOP ROW, full-body turnaround (calm pose, wings spread, chains of writing drifting around her):
  1. FULL BODY FRONT VIEW
  2. FULL BODY LEFT SIDE VIEW
  3. FULL BODY BACK VIEW (showing the back of the wings and the fluffy hair)
  4. FULL BODY RIGHT SIDE VIEW

MIDDLE ROW, head and shoulders studies (larger scale, detailed faces, ruff collar visible):
  5. HEAD FRONT VIEW
  6. HEAD THREE-QUARTER VIEW
  7. HEAD LEFT PROFILE
  8. HEAD RIGHT PROFILE
  9. HEAD BACK VIEW

EXPRESSION STRIP (small heads in a row):
  - AMUSED: the small calm doll smile.
  - HUNGRY: a wide grin full of small sharp teeth, eyes glowing brighter.
  - FURIOUS: no smile, eyes narrowed, hair gone still.
  - ANCIENT: a completely blank, cold, inhuman doll stare.

RIGHT SIDE, large feature panel:
  10. THE BANQUET HALL: she sits at the head of an endless dining table that stretches away into darkness, with a white tablecloth and thousands of EMPTY silver plates and empty chairs. Tall candles line the table, and their flames point DOWNWARD, the wrong way, all leaning toward her. Her feather gown spills over a tall black carved throne, her eye-covered wings fill the darkness behind her, and chains of writing drift through the air above the table. She looks at the viewer with her calm doll smile. Dark, moody lighting from the downward candles and her glowing gold eyes.

SMALL DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES (along the bottom edge):
  - Her eye close-up (dark ring, gold iris, violet ring).
  - The gold crescent-and-ring forehead mark.
  - One wing eye-spot close-up.
  - A section of the chain of writing.
  - The ruff collar and choker.
  - A color palette strip.

=== LIGHTING & COLOR ===
Dark, moody, eerie lighting with cool violet ambient light and deep shadows. Her gold eyes, forehead mark and wing eye-spots glow warmly. The palette is ivory, lavender, black-violet, plum and gold.

=== TEXT ===
A small clean title at the top: "ARCANULA — DEVIL OF GLUTTONY". Tiny labels under each view: "FRONT", "LEFT", "BACK", "RIGHT", "HEAD FRONT", "3/4", "LEFT PROFILE", "RIGHT PROFILE", "HEAD BACK", "AMUSED", "HUNGRY", "FURIOUS", "ANCIENT", "THE BANQUET HALL". All text spelled correctly. No other text.

=== QUALITY ===
Masterpiece quality, highly detailed, sharp clean lineart, consistent design across all views, symmetrical eyes, professional anime studio design sheet, 4K.

=== AVOID (STRICT) ===
Keep her fully covered by the feather gown from neck to feet: no bare skin other than the face, nothing revealing, tight or see-through, and no suggestive, flirty or seductive poses or expressions. Also avoid: extra characters, gore, a different design from the reference, black-and-white output, extra limbs, cropped heads, watermarks, signatures, misspelled text, chibi style, 3D render look and photorealism.
```

After the design is picked, the book's description of Arcanula will be

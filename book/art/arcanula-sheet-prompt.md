# Arcanula: character sheet prompt (for Gemini)

**Author's direction:** she should look almost exactly like Megicula from
Black Clover, with only small tweaks, and she must be fully and modestly
dressed.

**How to use it:**

1. Attach a clear picture of **Megicula** first. This is the design reference.
2. Attach the same art-style reference image used for Owen, Jonah and Talia
   second.
3. Paste the prompt below.

The prompt leans on the Megicula image for her face, hair, horns and overall
look, and changes only the listed tweaks. It doesn't name her, because Gemini
often refuses prompts that name existing characters. The attached picture does
that job instead. If the result isn't close enough, try adding "in the style
of Megicula from Black Clover" to the first line.

```
Create a professional anime CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET / MODEL SHEET of one character: ARCANULA, the Devil of Gluttony.

=== REFERENCES (VERY IMPORTANT) ===
The FIRST attached image is the DESIGN REFERENCE. Arcanula should look ALMOST EXACTLY like the character in the first image. Closely match her face shape, eyes, expression, hair (style, length, color and the way it flows), horns, facial markings, skin tone, body shape, height and overall demon silhouette. Keep her instantly recognizable as that design, and only apply the small TWEAKS listed below.
The SECOND attached image is the ART STYLE reference only: clean premium anime lineart, soft cel shading, muted elegant colors and faint paper grain. Do not copy its characters or clothes.

=== THE TWEAKS (ONLY CHANGE THESE) ===
1. MODEST, FULLY COVERED OUTFIT (MOST IMPORTANT): replace the reference's outfit completely. She wears a long, loose, flowing, FULL-LENGTH GOWN that covers her whole body:
   - A HIGH NECK up to the throat. No neckline opening at all.
   - LONG SLEEVES down to the wrists, loose and wide, flaring at the cuffs like bell sleeves.
   - Floor length, covering her legs and feet completely, falling in loose, wide folds. NOT tight or form-fitting anywhere.
   - No bare shoulders, no bare arms, no bare stomach, no bare legs, no slits, no cutouts, no sheer fabric.
   - Only her FACE and HANDS are visible.
   - Material: deep glossy BLACK like spilled ink, with a faint violet shimmer. The hem pools on the ground and ripples slowly on its own like ink.
   - Over the gown, a long, flowing black cloak or mantle with a tall, stiff, pointed queen's collar standing up behind her head, lined in deep violet.
   - Thin GOLD crescent-moon embroidery along the collar, cuffs and hem.
2. CRESCENT MOTIF: her facial markings keep the same placement as the reference, but are drawn as thin, glowing GOLD lines shaped like small CRESCENT MOONS. If the reference's horns can be curved into a crescent-moon arc without changing their overall look, do that.
3. EYES: black sclera with glowing GOLD irises and a thin violet ring around each iris.
4. BELT CLASP: a thin gold chain belt at her waist, over the gown, with one clasp shaped like a single CRESCENT MOON with a thin circular ring passing through it (like a small planet caught in a crescent's pull).
5. A thin gold circlet across her forehead with one small crescent in the center.
6. BLACK CLAWS on her fingers. Her hair drifts and floats slowly around her as if she were underwater.
Everything else stays as close to the first reference image as possible.

=== WHO SHE IS ===
An ancient, elegant, regal demon who has been locked in a magic book for sixty-one years. She's playful and teasing on the surface, but cold, ancient and menacing underneath. She should look like a dark queen: graceful, calm and frightening, never flirty or seductive.

=== SHEET LAYOUT ===
A wide landscape character sheet (16:9) on a dark charcoal-violet background with very faint grid lines, like a professional anime studio model sheet. Every view shows the SAME character in the SAME outfit, colors and proportions, hovering slightly above the ground at the same scale.

TOP ROW, full-body turnaround (calm, regal standing pose, hands folded in front of her or resting at her sides, a slight knowing smile):
  1. FULL BODY FRONT VIEW
  2. FULL BODY LEFT SIDE VIEW
  3. FULL BODY BACK VIEW
  4. FULL BODY RIGHT SIDE VIEW

MIDDLE ROW, head and shoulders studies (larger scale, detailed faces, high collar visible):
  5. HEAD FRONT VIEW
  6. HEAD THREE-QUARTER VIEW
  7. HEAD LEFT PROFILE
  8. HEAD RIGHT PROFILE
  9. HEAD BACK VIEW

EXPRESSION STRIP (small heads in a row):
  - AMUSED: a small, sly closed-mouth smile.
  - HUNGRY: a wide grin showing sharp teeth, eyes glowing brighter.
  - FURIOUS: eyes narrowed, no smile, hair gone still.
  - ANCIENT: a completely blank, cold, inhuman stare.

RIGHT SIDE, large feature panel:
  10. THE BANQUET HALL: she sits at the head of an endless dining table that stretches away into darkness, with a white tablecloth and thousands of EMPTY silver plates and empty chairs. Tall candles line the table, and their flames point DOWNWARD, the wrong way, all leaning toward her. She sits upright on a tall black carved chair like a throne, her clawed hands folded on the table, her gown and cloak spilling across the floor. She looks at the viewer with a calm, menacing smile. Dark, moody lighting from the downward candles and her glowing eyes.

SMALL DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES (along the bottom edge):
  - Her eye close-up (black sclera, gold iris, violet ring).
  - Her gold crescent facial marking.
  - The crescent-and-ring belt clasp.
  - Her clawed hand at the end of the long sleeve.
  - A color palette strip.

=== LIGHTING & COLOR ===
Dark, moody, elegant lighting with cool violet ambient light and deep shadows. Her gold eyes and markings glow warmly. The palette is black, violet, gold and grey, plus whatever skin and hair colors match the reference.

=== TEXT ===
A small clean title at the top: "ARCANULA — DEVIL OF GLUTTONY". Tiny labels under each view: "FRONT", "LEFT", "BACK", "RIGHT", "HEAD FRONT", "3/4", "LEFT PROFILE", "RIGHT PROFILE", "HEAD BACK", "AMUSED", "HUNGRY", "FURIOUS", "ANCIENT", "THE BANQUET HALL". All text spelled correctly. No other text.

=== QUALITY ===
Masterpiece quality, highly detailed, sharp clean lineart, consistent across all views, accurate anatomy, five clawed fingers per hand, symmetrical eyes, professional anime studio design sheet, 4K.

=== AVOID (STRICT) ===
No revealing clothing of any kind: no cleavage, no low neckline, no bare shoulders, arms, midriff or legs, no slits, no cutouts, no sheer or see-through fabric, no tight or body-hugging clothing, and no suggestive, flirty or seductive poses or expressions. Also avoid: extra characters, gore, bat wings, a tail, different outfits between views, extra fingers, distorted hands, cropped heads, watermarks, signatures, misspelled text, chibi style, 3D render look and photorealism.
```

After the design is picked, the book's description of Arcanula (skin, hair,
horns) will be updated to match the final image.

# Arcanula: character sheet prompt (for Gemini)

Attach the same art-style reference image used for Owen, Jonah and Talia
first. If you like, attach a picture of the Black Clover devil you want her to
resemble second. The prompt tells Gemini to use it only as a design-mood
reference.

The prompt doesn't name the Black Clover character. Gemini tends to refuse or
water down prompts that name existing characters. The description carries
that look (the long dark floating hair, the black-sclera eyes, the elegant
cruel devil-queen silhouette, the heavy ink shadows) while keeping her our
own character with her own horns, colors and crescent markings.

Create a professional anime CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET / MODEL SHEET of one original character: ARCANULA, an ancient female demon, the Devil of Gluttony who lives inside the Gravity griamore.

=== ART STYLE (VERY IMPORTANT) ===
Copy ONLY the ART STYLE of the FIRST attached reference image (clean premium anime lineart, soft cel shading, muted elegant colors, faint paper grain). Do not copy its characters, clothing or background.
If a SECOND image is attached, use it ONLY as a mood reference for the overall devil design: the long flowing dark hair, the elegant and cruel feminine silhouette, the dark-sclera eyes and the menacing smile. Do not copy it exactly. This is an original character.
Push the style DARKER than a normal character sheet, like a demon design from a dark shonen fantasy anime:
- Sharper, more angular lineart on her silhouette, claws, horns and gown edges, with thick black outer lines and very fine interior strokes on the face.
- Heavy, dramatic BLACK ink shadows with hard edges, especially under the hair, around the eyes and in the folds of the gown.
- Glowing features (eyes and markings) that stand out brightly against the dark shading.
- She should look beautiful, elegant and regal first, and terrifying second. The fear comes from her smile and her eyes.

=== WHO SHE IS ===
Arcanula is one of the oldest things in the world. She has been hungry since the world was mud. She is the demon of GLUTTONY, bound inside a forbidden magic book (a "griamore") of GRAVITY, and she wants to devour everything: food, magic, feelings, people, power. She has been locked away for sixty-one years in a dark, endless banquet hall of empty plates, and she has just made a deal with a 16-year-old boy. She is playful, affectionate and teasing ("darling"), then suddenly possessive and cold and ancient. She's a queen at a feast, and everyone else is on the menu.

=== BODY ===
- VERY TALL: at least 8 heads tall, towering and willowy, with impossibly long, elegant limbs and a slender waist. Graceful, like a dancer.
- Posture: relaxed, languid and confident. Her head is often tilted slightly, the way a predator looks at something interesting.
- Her feet never quite touch the ground. She hovers an inch above the floor, and the hem of her gown pools and spills below her.

=== SKIN ===
- BRUISE-GREY-VIOLET skin: a cool, pale grey with a deep violet undertone, darker (almost plum) at her fingertips, lips and around her eyes, like shadow soaked into the skin.
- Flawless and smooth, with a very faint, soft sheen.

=== FACE ===
- A long, elegant, sharply beautiful face with high cheekbones, a narrow pointed chin and a thin straight nose. Aristocratic and ageless.
- EYES (very important): fully BLACK SCLERA (the whites of the eyes are pure black), with bright GLOWING GOLD irises, each iris ringed with a thin band of VIOLET at the outer edge, and slit-like dark pupils. Long dark lashes. Heavy-lidded and half-closed, lazy and amused, as if she already knows how this ends.
- Dark plum lips curved in a slow, knowing, cruel smile.
- TEETH: when she smiles wide she has FAR TOO MANY TEETH, rows of small, neat, needle-sharp white teeth. In the neutral views her smile stays closed-lipped and just slightly too wide. In the expression panels her grin shows them.
- GOLD CRESCENT MARKINGS: thin, glowing gold tattoo-like markings shaped like small CRESCENT MOONS. One crescent sits under each eye on the upper cheek, curving like a tear, and a thin gold line runs from each down the cheek toward the jaw. More crescents and fine lines trace her collarbones, her shoulders, and down the outside of both arms to her wrists. They glow softly like embers.

=== HORNS ===
- Two BLACK, RIDGED horns rising from just above her temples and sweeping BACK over her head in a smooth arc, each shaped like a CRESCENT MOON with the points aiming backward. They are glossy black with fine horizontal ridges, and the tips catch a faint gold highlight.

=== HAIR ===
- Extremely LONG, straight, INK-BLACK hair falling past her knees, with a faint deep-violet sheen in the highlights.
- IT FLOATS: her hair drifts and spreads slowly around her as if she were UNDERWATER, spreading out in long wavy strands that curl at the ends and fade into wisps of black shadow. This is her signature look.
- Long straight bangs parted at the center, framing her face, with two long locks falling in front of her shoulders.

=== HANDS ===
- Long, slender, elegant grey-violet fingers ending in long, sharp, glossy BLACK CLAWS, like black lacquered talons.
- Her hands are always doing something graceful: a claw raised as if beckoning, fingers folded under her chin, or one claw tracing a line.

=== OUTFIT: THE INK GOWN ===

- A high, dramatic collar that rises behind her neck in sharp points, like a queen's collar.
- A fitted, sleeveless bodice with a deep, elegant neckline (tasteful, not revealing), bordered in thin GOLD crescent filigree.

- The skirt falls long and wide and POOLS on the floor around her like a spreading ink spill. Its edges ripple and MOVE ON THEIR OWN, and thin tendrils of ink creep outward across the ground.
- At her waist, a thin gold chain belt with one clasp shaped like the GRAVITY EMBLEM: a single crescent moon with a thin circular ring passing through it, like a small planet caught in the pull of a crescent.
- A thin gold circlet across her forehead, under her bangs, with one small crescent at the center.
- Small gold crescent earrings.

=== AURA ===
- Faint black-violet shadow drifts off her edges constantly, like smoke off a candle.
- Tiny objects (a silver fork, a crumb, a candle flame, a pearl) float slowly toward her, as if she's pulling everything in.

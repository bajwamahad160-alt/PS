```
Create a professional anime CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET / MODEL SHEET of one original character: JONAH PIKE, age 16, the THUNDER GRANDMASTER.

=== ART STYLE (VERY IMPORTANT) ===
Copy ONLY the ART STYLE of the attached reference image. Do not copy its characters, clothing, or background.
The style is a modern premium anime illustration, like a high-end light novel cover or anime key visual:
- Semi-realistic anime proportions for a 16-year-old boy (about 7 heads tall), tall and lanky, natural, not chibi and not exaggerated.
- Thin, clean, confident lineart in dark brown/black with slight line-weight variation: thicker outer silhouette, thinner interior lines, extremely fine strokes on the face.
- Soft cel shading with 2-3 shadow tones, gently blended at the edges, and a subtle cool blue rim light.
- Large, expressive anime eyes with layered iris gradients, two bright catchlights, and defined upper lashes.
- Hair drawn in soft chunky locks that separate into fine strands at the tips, with gentle glossy highlights and slight color variation.
- Fair, light skin with a light pink blush on the cheeks and nose, and freckles.
- Slightly muted, desaturated color grading: warm highlights, cool shadows, nothing neon.
- A faint paper/grain texture over the whole image and light hatching in fabric shadows, so it feels half painted and half drawn.
- Believable fabric with real weight and natural folds.
- A friendly, alive face with a genuine expression.

=== WHO HE IS ===
Jonah is a working-class kid from the harbor docks of a 1920s-style magical city, and the class clown: loud, funny, loyal, always joking, with a huge heart. He never wanted to be famous. He wanted an ordinary water power and a quiet job on the canals. Then, on his sixteenth birthday, he became the THUNDER GRANDMASTER, one of the seven most powerful people in the world. He's excited and terrified at the same time. His design should feel goofy and warm on the surface, with real storm power underneath.

=== BODY ===
- Age 16. Tall and LANKY: long arms and legs, a bit gangly, still growing into himself. Relaxed, loose posture, like he can never quite stand still.
- The tallest of his friend group.

=== FACE ===
- He is white, with FAIR, LIGHT, freckled skin.
- A friendly, open, slightly long face with a pointed chin and big expressive eyebrows that move a lot.
- Eyes: bright STORM-BLUE eyes (deep navy at the edges, fading to bright electric blue near the pupil), very detailed, with sharp white catchlights like tiny lightning glints.
- LOTS of freckles across his nose and cheeks.
- A big, wide, cheeky GRIN with straight, even white teeth.
- Expression in the neutral views: a relaxed, mischievous grin.

=== HAIR ===
- Scruffy ASH-BROWN hair with lighter sandy tips, medium length and fully visible. It STICKS UP in random directions from static electricity, fluffy, a little frizzy and spiky, with a few strands falling over his forehead. It looks like he just touched a live wire, which he probably did.
- Small tiny sparks occasionally flicker at the tips of a few strands.

=== OUTFIT: GREY AND DARK BLUE GRANDMASTER SUIT ===
A cool, stylish, tailored suit that mixes a 1920s Art Deco look with modern anime hero design. Mostly STORM GREY with DARK NAVY BLUE, with silver and a few gold details (because he's a Grandmaster):
- Jacket: a storm-grey tailored suit jacket, slightly loose on his lanky frame, cut to the hip with sharp angled front hems. Thin silver ZIGZAG Art Deco piping (like lightning) runs along the lapels, cuffs and hems. A double row of silver buttons. The sleeves are always pushed up to his elbows because he hates stiff sleeves. A GOLD THUNDER EMBLEM (placeholder: a bold zigzag lightning bolt) is stitched on the LEFT chest, like a badge of honor, since this is the Thunder Grandmaster's mark.
- Shirt: a dark navy-blue shirt with the top button undone.
- Suspenders: dark navy suspenders with silver clips, visible under the open jacket, as a nod to his dock-worker roots.
- Scarf: a long DARK BLUE knitted scarf with a subtle grey zigzag pattern, wrapped loosely once around his neck, with the ends hanging down to his waist and flicking in the wind.
- Belt: a slim black belt with a silver buckle shaped like a lightning bolt.
- Trousers: dark navy tailored trousers, slightly rolled at the ankles, with a thin silver stripe down the outer seams.
- Gloves: grey fingerless leather gloves with small SILVER STUDS across the knuckles that spark when he uses magic.
- Boots: dark navy lace-up boots with silver toe caps and grey laces, a little scuffed.
- MINI CAPE: a short storm-cloud MANTLE that covers BOTH shoulders and ends at mid-back, like a short cloak collar. Storm-grey outside, deep DARK NAVY satin lining inside, trimmed with a silver zigzag Art Deco border. It is fastened at the center of his chest with a round silver clasp shaped like a lightning bolt. The edges of the mantle ripple as if caught in a storm wind.
- The whole outfit looks cool and heroic, like a famous champion's uniform, but worn in a messy, relaxed, very Jonah way.

=== HIS GRIAMORE (MAGIC BOOK) ===
- An EXTREMELY THICK magic book, as thick as a dictionary, with EVERY page filled. A dark storm-blue/slate leather cover with silver metal corner guards and silver-blue gilded page edges.
- A large GOLD THUNDER EMBLEM (placeholder: a bold zigzag lightning bolt) is embossed in the center of the cover, with faint blue sparks crawling around its edges.
- When not in use, it hangs on his right hip in a grey leather book holster with navy stitching.
- In the magic pose it FLOATS on its own in mid-air, open, above his head, with pages flipping by themselves.

=== SHEET LAYOUT ===
A wide landscape character sheet (16:9) on a clean warm off-white/light parchment background with very faint grid lines, like a professional anime studio model sheet. Every view shows the SAME character with the SAME outfit, colors, proportions and height. Full-body figures stand on the same baseline at the same scale.

TOP ROW, full-body turnaround in a neutral standing pose (relaxed, hands loosely at his sides, weight on one leg, easy grin):
  1. FULL BODY FRONT VIEW: facing the viewer directly, showing the static hair, the scarf, the mantle and clasp, the gold Thunder emblem on his chest, the suspenders, and the book holster on his right hip.
  2. FULL BODY LEFT SIDE VIEW: exact left profile, showing his lanky silhouette, the scarf ends, the spiky hair silhouette, and the rolled-up sleeves.
  3. FULL BODY BACK VIEW: facing away, showing the back of the storm-grey mantle with its silver zigzag trim, the scarf ends, and the staticky hair sticking up at the back.
  4. FULL BODY RIGHT SIDE VIEW: exact right profile, showing the book holster and the gloves with silver studs.

MIDDLE ROW, head and shoulders studies (larger scale, detailed faces):
  5. HEAD FRONT VIEW: facing forward with a big cheeky grin. Storm-blue eyes clearly detailed.
  6. HEAD THREE-QUARTER VIEW: turned about 45 degrees, laughing, eyebrows raised.
  7. HEAD LEFT SIDE PROFILE: exact left profile, showing the spiky static hair silhouette and his pointed chin.
  8. HEAD RIGHT SIDE PROFILE: exact right profile, showing the scarf wrapped around his neck.
  9. HEAD BACK VIEW: the back of his head, showing the fluffy static hair sticking up in every direction.

RIGHT SIDE / BOTTOM, large feature panel:
  10. GRIAMORE MAGIC POSE: a dynamic, energetic full-body pose. His griamore floats open in the air ABOVE his head, with pages flipping wildly. THUNDER MAGIC explodes from it: bright blue-white lightning bolts arc from the open pages down to his raised hand, small dark storm clouds swirl around the book, and electric sparks jump between his gloved knuckle studs. His other hand is clenched into a fist at his side, crackling with sparks. His hair stands completely straight up with static, his scarf and mantle whip wildly in the wind, and sparks run along his arms. His expression is a huge, wild, excited grin, half thrilled and half "oh no," with his storm-blue eyes glowing bright electric blue. Tiny blue sparks crackle around his boots on the ground.

SMALL DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES (along the bottom edge):
  - A close-up of the griamore cover showing the gold Thunder emblem with blue sparks.
  - A close-up of the lightning-bolt mantle clasp.
  - A close-up of the fingerless glove with silver knuckle studs.
  - A close-up of a navy boot.
  - A color palette strip with swatches: storm grey, charcoal grey, dark navy blue, electric blue, silver, gold, ash-brown (hair), storm blue (eyes), fair freckled skin tone.

=== LIGHTING & COLOR ===
Soft, even studio lighting for the turnaround views so every detail is clear. Bright blue-white electric light only in the griamore magic pose, with dramatic flickering highlights and deep storm shadows. The main palette is storm grey and dark navy blue, with silver and small gold accents, and his fair freckled skin and ash-brown hair as contrast. The colors stay slightly muted and elegant, matching the reference style.

=== TEXT ===
At the top of the sheet, a clean, small title: "JONAH PIKE — THUNDER GRANDMASTER". Tiny clean labels under each view: "FRONT", "LEFT", "BACK", "RIGHT", "HEAD FRONT", "3/4", "LEFT PROFILE", "RIGHT PROFILE", "HEAD BACK", "GRIAMORE". All text spelled correctly. No other text.

=== QUALITY ===
Masterpiece quality, highly detailed, sharp clean lineart, consistent character across all views, accurate anatomy, correct hands with five fingers, symmetrical eyes, professional anime studio character design sheet, 4K resolution.

=== AVOID ===
Do not include: extra characters, different outfits between views, inconsistent hair, hats, caps, missing teeth, gaps between teeth, extra fingers, distorted hands, cropped feet, cropped heads, messy background, watermarks, signatures, misspelled text, chibi style, 3D render look, photorealism, neon colors, purple or white suits.
```

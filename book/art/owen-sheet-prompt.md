=== ART STYLE (VERY IMPORTANT) ===
Copy ONLY the ART STYLE of the FIRST attached reference image. Do not copy its characters, clothing, or background.
The SECOND attached image is Owen's secret emblem (see EMBLEMS below). Copy its exact shape.
The style is a modern premium anime illustration, like a high-end light novel cover or anime key visual:
- Semi-realistic anime proportions for a 16-year-old boy (about 7 heads tall), lean and natural, not chibi and not exaggerated.
- Thin, clean, confident lineart in dark brown/black with slight line-weight variation: thicker outer silhouette, thinner interior lines, extremely fine strokes on the face.
- Soft cel shading with 2-3 shadow tones, gently blended at the edges, and a subtle cool rim light.
- Large, expressive anime eyes with layered iris gradients, two bright catchlights, and defined upper lashes.
- Hair drawn in soft chunky locks that separate into fine strands at the tips, with gentle glossy highlights and slight color variation.
- Warm skin with a light blush on the cheeks and nose and a few faint freckles.
- Slightly muted, desaturated color grading: warm highlights, cool shadows, nothing neon.
- A faint paper/grain texture over the whole image and light hatching in fabric shadows, so it feels half painted and half drawn.
- Believable fabric with real weight and natural folds.
- A friendly, alive face with a genuine expression.

=== WHO HE IS ===
Owen is a working-class kid from the harbor docks of a 1920s-style magical city. He's the underdog of the story: quiet, loyal, a little unsure of himself, but stubborn and brave when it counts. His two best friends became famous champions, and he's trying to keep up. He believes his magic book holds Gravity, a rare power. Secretly, it's something far bigger and darker, and he doesn't know it yet. His design should feel humble and grounded, with a hidden, dangerous edge.

=== EMBLEMS (VERY IMPORTANT) ===
Owen has two emblems. One is hidden inside the other.

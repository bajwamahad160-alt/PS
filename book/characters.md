# Characters: *The Forgotten Seven*

Items marked *(proposed)* are drafting suggestions and still need the
author's approval.

## Owen: main character

A guy named Owen. Everything past that is still open.

*(proposed)*

- **Age:** 16, the age when you get your griamore at the Bindery.
- **Background:** grew up working-class on the docks of the big city.
  Knows every Grandmaster's stats off the trading cards and assumed he'd
  end up with a small, ordinary fire griamore like his dad.
- **The hook:** at the Bindery, his griamore's logo inks itself onto the
  cover, and the clerk can't find it in the registry. Nobody can. It
  belongs to one of the Demonic Seven, which no one alive remembers.
- **Which book?** Still to be decided. **Time** would be a strong starter:
  it's flashy, easy to show on the page, and pairs with Sloth, which makes
  an interesting sin for a restless kid to fight against. **Reqrium** is
  the kind of power to save for the endgame.

## Supporting cast

*(proposed, loose placeholders until Owen is locked)*

- **A Grandmaster.** One of the Elemental Seven gets drawn into Owen's
  story, as a mentor, a rival, or the person hunting him.
- **Another demonic holder.** Someone who got their forgotten book before
  Owen did and knows far more than they're saying.
- **A friend from the docks.** Has an ordinary griamore and is Owen's anchor
  to normal life.
- **The Bindery.** The institution itself, which might know more about the
  forgotten seven than it admits.
